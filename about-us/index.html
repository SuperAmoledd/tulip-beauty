TULIP BEAUTY

LASHES. BROWS. ACADEMY.

Our beauty salon was established in 2019, and since then have endeavoured to deliver personalized and professional beauty services that help our customers achieve their beauty goals.

Our highly qualified beauticians has over a decade of experiences between them with certifications to match, adhering to a healthy hygienic practices and fully insured for your peace of mind. Professionalism would be an understatement and we strive to impress, over and over again.

We specialise in Eye Lash Extensions; from Classic to Russian, Cosmetic Tattoo including Microblading tattoo for Brows, Lips and Scalp, Laser Hair Removal and Waxing.

We pride ourselves in giving you a relaxing experience and committed to perform and conduct your treatments in a safe and professional environment and of course WOW results every time for days/months to come! Are you ready to be impressed? Don’t hesitate. Book Now!
