About Waxing & Tinting

Our tints are custom-blended to complement your natural hair color and skin tone perfectly, creating fuller-looking brows and lashes. Results typically last between 3 to 4 weeks depending on your hair cycle.

Please note: If you are using any retinol, AHA/BHA, or acne medication, please inform your technician prior to waxing to prevent skin lifting or irritation.
