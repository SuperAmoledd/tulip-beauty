About Brow Lamination

To ensure your brows stay healthy and the results last, we highly recommend keeping them completely dry and untouched for the first 24 hours after your treatment. Avoid steam, heavy sweating, and makeup on the brow area during this period.

Please note: Daily conditioning with a specialized brow serum or castor oil is essential to keep the hairs hydrated and flexible after lamination.
